Why I built this

I spent three years on the builder side, running 5 new construction communities in Utah County. The most common mistake I saw was buyers who didn't know their budget before they walked in.

They'd fall in love with a floor plan at base price. Then the lot premium, structural upgrades, and design center selections were added in, and they had priced themselves out. The next step was cutting the things they loved most.

Knowing your real number first means you only tour homes that fit, and you walk into the design center with a plan.
